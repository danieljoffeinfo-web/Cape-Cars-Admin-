CAPE CARS RENTAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Please read these terms carefully before accepting the booking in Telegram. By tapping Accept, you confirm that you understand and agree to the rental conditions below.

1. Driver requirements
The main driver must be at least 23 years old and must have held a valid driver's license for at least 2 years. Cape Cars may refuse handover if the driver does not meet these requirements or if the documents provided are unclear, invalid, expired, or do not match the person collecting the vehicle.

2. Required documents
Before a vehicle is released, the customer must provide a clear passport or ID image, the front and back of the driver's license, a working WhatsApp or phone number, and proof of the booking deposit payment. Cape Cars may request the original documents at collection.

3. Booking deposit and payment
A booking deposit of 5000 RUB is payable to secure the reservation. The remaining rental balance is due upfront on collection, before the vehicle is handed over. If payment is not received or cannot be verified, Cape Cars may delay or refuse vehicle release.

4. Collection and delivery
Standard handover is at 21 Montague Drive, Montague Gardens, Cape Town, 7441. Airport delivery is available at $100 each way. After-hours delivery or return is charged at double the normal delivery or return rate and must be arranged with a manager in advance.

5. Vehicle return
The vehicle must be returned on the agreed date and time, clean, with the same fuel level as at handover, and in the same condition except for fair wear. Late return is charged at 1000 ZAR. A delay of more than 3 hours may be charged as an additional rental day.

6. Use restrictions
Smoking is not allowed in the vehicle. The vehicle may not be used for taxi work, delivery work, racing, off-road driving, illegal activity, towing, or by any driver not approved by Cape Cars. Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs is strictly prohibited.

7. Accidents, damage, and fines
If there is an accident, damage, theft, warning light, mechanical issue, traffic fine, or police stop, the customer must contact the Cape Cars manager immediately. In an accident, the customer must obtain a police case number and share all available details with Cape Cars.

8. Cancellation
If the customer cancels less than 5 days before the rental start date, the prepayment is non-refundable. Cape Cars may cancel or amend a booking if required documents are not supplied, payment is not verified, or the vehicle becomes unavailable due to circumstances outside Cape Cars' reasonable control.

9. Acceptance
Tap Accept in Telegram only if you agree to these terms. After acceptance, Cape Cars will send the bank details for the 5000 RUB booking deposit.
