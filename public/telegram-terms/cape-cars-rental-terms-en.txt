CAR RENTAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS

⸻

1. Driver requirements

Driver age: from 23 years old
Driving experience: from 2 years
Required:
passport
driver's license
WhatsApp contact number
International driver's license — if necessary

⸻

2. Vehicle booking procedure

Vehicle selection

The client selects a vehicle from the catalog and agrees the rental dates with the manager.

⸻

To confirm the booking, the following is required:

make a booking prepayment — 5000 ₽
send:
passport photo
photo of the driver's license (both sides)
WhatsApp contact number
payment confirmation

⸻

Booking confirmation

After receiving payment and documents, the client is entered into the booking system.

The client is sent:

booking confirmation
booking screenshot
agreed vehicle collection terms

⸻

3. Vehicle collection and return

Vehicle collection at the office is available from 10:00 in the morning.

📍 Office address:
21 Montague Drive, Montague Gardens, Cape Town, 7441

📍 Map location:
https://www.google.com/maps?q=21+Montague+Drive,+Montague+Gardens,+Cape+Town,+7441

Vehicle return is made:

on the rental end date
no later than 10:00 in the morning, unless otherwise agreed in advance

Vehicle collection and return on weekends must be agreed in advance, at least 3 working days before.

⸻

4. Vehicle delivery

It is necessary to advise in advance:

whether delivery is required
vehicle delivery address
collection time
whether airport delivery is required

All deliveries are planned in advance.

⸻

Delivery cost

Cape Town International Airport

vehicle delivery — 100$
vehicle return — 100$

⸻

5. Outside working hours

If vehicle delivery or return takes place outside working hours, the service cost is doubled.

This rule applies to:

vehicle delivery
vehicle return
airport delivery and return
collection or return of the vehicle outside working hours

⸻

Office working hours

Weekdays

10:00 — 17:00

Saturday

10:00 — 13:00

Sunday

closed

⸻

6. Vehicle condition

The vehicle is handed over to the client:

in clean condition
with the fuel level recorded

The client must return the vehicle:

in clean condition
with the same fuel level

⸻

Vehicle wash

If the vehicle is returned dirty:

additional wash — 300 ZAR

If the interior is heavily soiled, the dry-cleaning cost is calculated separately.

⸻

7. Fuel

The vehicle is returned with the same amount of fuel with which it was received:

if the vehicle was issued with ¼ tank — return with ¼ tank
if the vehicle was issued with a full tank — return with a full tank

Missing fuel is deducted from the deposit.

⸻

8. Deposit

The deposit amount depends on the vehicle class.

The deposit is held for the rental period and until the vehicle is checked after return.

The deposit is returned immediately after:

checking the vehicle condition
payment for additional services
absence of outstanding debts

⸻

9. Late return

late return — 1000 ZAR
delay of more than 3 hours — charged as an additional rental day

⸻

10. Fines and deductions

In case of:

refusal to return the vehicle
ignoring communication
breach of rental terms

a fine of 100% of the deposit is withheld.

⸻

11. Additional fines

smoked interior — 200$
wheel rim damage or scratches — 150–400$
body scratches — from 350$ (depending on the extent of damage)

The cost is determined after vehicle inspection.

⸻

12. Traffic fines

Traffic fines may arrive up to 8 months after the rental.

If a fine is received:

the client is sent information about the fine (date and time of violation);
it is confirmed that the fine was received during the client's rental period;
the client must pay the fine by money transfer.

All fine expenses are borne by the renter.

⸻

13. Prohibited

transferring the vehicle to third parties
using it for taxi or delivery without agreement
participating in racing, drifting
driving under the influence
violating South African law
