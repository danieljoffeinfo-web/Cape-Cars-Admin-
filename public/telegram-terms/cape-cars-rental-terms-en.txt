CAR RENTAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. Driver Requirements

Driver age: from 23 years old.
Driving experience: at least 2 years.

The driver must provide:
- passport;
- driver's license;
- WhatsApp contact number.

An international driving permit may be required where necessary.

2. Vehicle Booking Process

Vehicle selection

The client chooses a vehicle from the catalogue and agrees the rental dates with the manager.

To confirm the booking, the client must:
- pay the booking prepayment of 5000 RUB;
- send a passport photo;
- send photos of both sides of the driver's license;
- send a WhatsApp contact number;
- send proof of payment.

Booking confirmation

After payment and documents are received, the client is entered into the booking system.

The client receives:
- booking confirmation;
- a screenshot of the booking;
- the agreed vehicle collection conditions.

3. Vehicle Collection and Return

Vehicles are released from the office from 10:00 in the morning.

Office address:
21 Montague Drive, Montague Gardens, Cape Town, 7441

Map location:
https://www.google.com/maps?q=21+Montague+Drive,+Montague+Gardens,+Cape+Town,+7441

The vehicle must be returned:
- on the rental end date;
- no later than 10:00 in the morning, unless agreed otherwise in advance.

Weekend vehicle collection and return must be agreed in advance, at least 3 working days before.

4. Vehicle Delivery

The client must advise in advance:
- whether delivery is required;
- the delivery address;
- collection time;
- whether airport delivery is required.

All deliveries are planned in advance.

Delivery cost

Cape Town International Airport:
- vehicle delivery — 100$;
- vehicle return — 100$.

5. Outside Working Hours

If vehicle delivery or return takes place outside working hours, the service cost is doubled.

This rule applies to:
- vehicle delivery;
- vehicle return;
- airport delivery and return;
- collection or drop-off outside office working hours.

Office working hours

Weekdays: 10:00 — 17:00.
Saturday: 10:00 — 13:00.
Sunday: closed.

6. Vehicle Condition

The vehicle is handed over to the client:
- clean;
- with the fuel level recorded.

The client must return the vehicle:
- clean;
- with the same fuel level.

Vehicle washing

If the vehicle is returned dirty:
- additional wash — 300 ZAR.

If the interior is heavily soiled, deep-cleaning cost is calculated separately.

7. Fuel

The vehicle must be returned with the same amount of fuel as it had at handover:
- if the vehicle is issued with ¼ tank, it must be returned with ¼ tank;
- if the vehicle is issued with a full tank, it must be returned with a full tank.

Missing fuel is deducted from the deposit.

8. Deposit

The deposit amount depends on the vehicle class.

The deposit is held during the rental period and until the vehicle is inspected after return.

The deposit is returned immediately after:
- vehicle condition inspection;
- payment of any additional services;
- confirmation that there are no outstanding amounts.

9. Late Return

- late return — 1000 ZAR;
- delay of more than 3 hours — charged as an additional rental day.

10. Penalties and Deductions

In the event of:
- refusal to return the vehicle;
- ignoring communication;
- breach of rental terms

a penalty of 100% of the deposit is withheld.

11. Additional Penalties

- smoked-in interior — 200$;
- wheel rim damage or scratches — 150–400$;
- body scratches — from 350$, depending on the level of damage.

The cost is determined after vehicle inspection.

12. Traffic Fines

Traffic fines may arrive up to 8 months after the rental.

If a fine is received:
- the client is sent the fine information, including the date and time of the violation;
- it is confirmed that the fine was received during the client's rental period;
- the client must pay the fine by money transfer.

All fine-related expenses are the renter's responsibility.

13. Prohibited Use

It is prohibited to:
- transfer the vehicle to third parties;
- use the vehicle for taxi or delivery work without approval;
- participate in racing or drifting;
- drive under the influence;
- violate South African law.

14. Accidents

In the event of a road accident, the renter must:
- stop immediately in a safe place;
- contact the Cape Cars manager;
- call the police or report the accident according to South African legal requirements;
- obtain a police case number if required;
- take photos and videos of the accident scene, damage, and participants' documents;
- not admit fault or sign documents without manager approval.

All damage, expenses, penalties, and deductions are calculated after the vehicle inspection and review of the accident circumstances.
